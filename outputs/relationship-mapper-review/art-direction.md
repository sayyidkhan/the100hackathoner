# Zo Relationship Mapper — opportunity atlas

Reviewed https://github.com/sayyidkhan/zo-relationship-mapper (README, src/App.tsx, src/types/api.ts and src/styles.css) and https://www.instagram.com/p/DaFSqK7k2ce/ including its product screenshots. The live deployment was blocked by the browser, so no live search was run.

The product parses a profile, ranks live opportunities, discovers people only after selecting a role, groups hiring managers/mentors/role colleagues, ranks contact paths and drafts contextual outreach. The exhibit compresses these into four illustrative steps. It does not present the illustrated contacts or routes as real search results or established personal connections.

Artwork generated using the built-in imagegen tool. Saved asset: public/landing/assets/projects/070-relationship-mapper-atlas-v2.png. Both page themes use the same emerald studio artwork and exhibit palette.

## Final image prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: premium editorial 3D hero artwork for Zo Relationship Mapper, a career opportunity and public-contact research tool. Landscape 3:2. A beautifully art-directed physical relationship map as a precision instrument on an extremely thin rounded dark emerald glass and brushed brass board, floating subtly in a deep forest-green studio. Sophisticated product photography, exceptionally tactile materials, cinematic softbox lighting, pearl-white ceramic, smoked jade glass, machined brass, delicate amber illumination. Composition reads from left to right: a large upright pearl profile card on left with refined sculpted human cameo, subtle engraved lines and three skill lozenges; in center three staggered upright opportunity cards like architectural monoliths, selected front card bordered in luminous brass with a small elegant office-building relief and three tiny match indicators; on right three smaller elevated portrait medallions arranged as a branching network representing hiring manager, mentor and role colleague, distinct abstract cameo faces; a beautifully folded pearl letter in the front right corner on a slim jade tray represents the contextual outreach draft. Extremely thin luminous amber paths are inlaid into the board connecting the profile to selected opportunity, branching to the contact medallions, with one selected amber route continuing to the letter. Other paths are dim teal. Fine concentric node markings, tiny machined connection terminals and precise topographic-style etched lines on the base make it feel like an exquisite instrument for discovering a path. No conventional bridge or river. Scene fills 85% width, isometric front three-quarter view, balanced spacing, gentle depth of field but all primary elements readable. Elegant, architectural and warm, high-end technology brand, detailed realistic bevels and contact shadows. Do not make a dashboard screenshot; this is a physical sculptural metaphor. No people names, fake company logos, percentages, numeric scores, text, lettering, watermarks. Avoid red, saturated cobalt blue, thick cables, giant display pedestal, toy figurines, bland beige tabletop.

## Implementation

Three.js jade and brass deck, profile cameo, ranked role cards, three labelled contact portraits, an outreach envelope, illuminated route inlays and moving signal points. Four manual steps and automatic 24-second cycle use the existing pause and reduced-motion handling.
