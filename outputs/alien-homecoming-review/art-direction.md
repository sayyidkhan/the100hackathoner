## Final image prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: premium cinematic game key art for Alien Homecoming, landscape 3:2. Create a breathtaking polished painterly 3D fantasy illustration of a tiny lavender alien lost between worlds, grounded in a game about collecting three Home Echoes and finding home. Vast deep midnight plum and indigo cosmos with delicate lavender rose nebulae, fine stars, an elegant distant ringed planet in upper left. Three beautifully crafted pale lilac marble floating islands, tapered fractured crystalline undersides, connected by impossibly graceful curved staircases and arched luminous walkways. Hero gateway on the rear right island: tall elegant circular-topped carved marble arch, nested bronze rings and glowing golden peach light through its opening, glimpses of a tiny warm home beyond. On the rear left island a delicate ornate brass telescope and an ivory tree with living glowing constellations. Foreground island holds the small lovable lavender pear-shaped alien with two dark shiny eyes and a single golden tipped antenna, facing the viewer in three-quarter profile, and three tiny warm luminous crystal memories along its path. Strong depth, architectural scale, visual storytelling, harmonious coherent layout, magical but restrained. Alien is small relative to architecture, not a mascot dominating the image. Marble, patinated brass, rose-gold highlights, crystalline rock, subtle carved concentric floor patterns, bioluminescent lavender foliage. Rich cinematic indirect lighting and atmospheric perspective, soft luminous bloom, high-end game art. Compose scene across frame with islands filling 80 percent width, no pedestal, no floor, suspended in space. Avoid red gates, cobalt tubes, beige tabletop craft, cartoon low-poly primitives, oversaturated rainbow, excessive particles. No lettering, text, logos, UI or watermark.

## Interactive interpretation

Procedural Three.js marble islands, curved elevated bridges with brass railings, star tree, telescope, emissive portal, ringed planet, firefly motes and three Home Echoes. A deterministic 36-second journey supports the existing pause, reset and reduced-motion controls. This is a conceptual animated exhibit; Play the game opens the original playable project.
