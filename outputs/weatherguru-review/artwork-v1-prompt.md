Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: 2D concept artwork for Weather Guru, hackathon #15, for user review before any website integration or interactive 3D implementation.

Create a beautifully art-directed, high-fidelity landscape 3:2 editorial product illustration for WEATHER GURU, a 2018 prototype that explores rainy-day patterns, rainfall magnitude, clusters and flood likelihood. Render a tactile miniature weather observatory: realistic materials and carefully crafted architectural details, sophisticated museum exhibit photography, not a toy render or generic dashboard.

Composition: a single coherent rectangular slice of a river catchment, presented at a low three-quarter angle. The landscape fills most of the image, resting directly on a dark blue-grey work surface with a thin exposed geological cross-section. At rear left, terraced green hills and a handful of finely detailed trees feed a winding glassy blue river. The river descends through the centre toward the foreground and passes a small refined neighbourhood on its right bank, with several distinct cream masonry homes, weathered terracotta roofs, tiny windows, a small footbridge and rain-darkened paved walkways. A low riverbank has shallow translucent water pooling across its lower edge: a restrained, legible flood-analysis metaphor, no destruction or people in danger. Include one elegant river-level measuring post with fine physical tick marks. The river and wet stone must have excellent water reflections and realistic depth.

Above the upper catchment at left floats one sculptural storm-cloud installation in layered frosted smoke-grey glass; individually resolved slender rain streaks descend toward the hills. The cloud is dramatic but not cartoon puffy spheres. A slender calibrated cylindrical rain gauge nearby connects the physical weather scene to measurement.

At rear right, behind the neighbourhood but with a clear silhouette, one thin upright smoked-glass analysis panel, in a refined dark metal frame. Precise minimal graphics: a compact rainfall bar chart across the upper portion, a carefully composed small treemap of green, muted ochre and coral rectangles below, and a thin rising line. These chart marks are clearly secondary to the landscape. Restrict panel typography to two large legible labels: "RAINFALL" and "FLOOD ANALYSIS". No percentages, fabricated statistics, live alerts, stock weather temperatures or dense UI. At front left, a small brushed metal inset nameplate says exactly "WEATHER GURU", with a simple cloud mark split into vertical bars as a nod to the original logo.

Materials: textured pale limestone edges, dark graphite anodized metal, lush moss-green terrain, transparent blue water, real fine trees, wet stone, terracotta roofs, restrained amber instrument lights. Palette is storm blue, slate, limestone cream, sage and small warm amber highlights. Elegant overcast daylight from upper left and warm window glints, rich but controlled contrast. Deep slate studio background, subtle atmospheric softness behind the cloud, crisp architectural and water detail throughout the object. Natural contact shadows, believable microtexture, restrained depth of field; all key elements sharp.

Keep the composition spacious and visually connected, strong distinctive silhouette, no oversized smartphone, no giant laptop, no gold luxury plinth, no ornamental arch, no unrelated mascot, no giant typography over image, no infographic arrows, no watermark. Everything fully inside the frame with comfortable edge margins. This is a polished static illustration, not UI code or an interactive 3D scene.
