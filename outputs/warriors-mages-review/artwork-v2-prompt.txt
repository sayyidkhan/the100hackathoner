Use case: stylized-concept

Each on an ivory round pedestal with a slim brass rim. Props are LARGE and legible, not tiny icons.
Front CENTRE between the two front destinations, a small partially open navy savings chest with polished brass hardware and a few gold coins, labelled SAVINGS. A short stream of three brass coins arcs from the central budget compass into the chest, conveying allocation.
Two small flat round persona tokens, knight-helmet silhouette and wizard-hat silhouette, are subtly inlaid near the book spine beneath the phone. No giant medallions or towering trophies.
Lighting: atmospheric midnight blue background with a subtle warm pool of light on the parchment, warm key from upper left, cool blue rim and soft realistic occlusion under all objects. Fine visible paper fibres, subtle engraved map contour lines on the book, blue leather grain, realistic ceramic glazes and metallic reflections. Restrained magical sparkle only on the active budget route. Photoreal crafted materials, sophisticated editorial taste, no cartoon faces or toys.
Text on front bookmark: "Warriors & Mages". Do not add ornamental text outside the scene. No people, no city skyline, no fake banking claims. Keep a clear hierarchy: PHONE first, BOOK second, budgeting props third.
