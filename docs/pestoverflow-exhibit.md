# PestOverflow exhibit

Source evidence: see [23/27 research](exhibits-23-27-research.md), including pinned README and Python source. The Raspberry Pi/PIR/SMS prototype is not represented as a deployed detection system. Camera hardware comes from the README concept; animation does not imply a working image classifier.

Composition: brick utility wall and brass pipework left; open ivory enclosure with green board, connector pins, ports, sensor dome, camera and wires centre; SMS phone with location relief right. A small mouse follows a fixed floor route. Warm mineral surfaces and studio lighting match the artwork.

Animation: 14-second approach, sensor indication, a single notification pulse, SMS hold, eased turn/exit, rest. No camera orbit or unrelated motion. Uses the shared pause/reduced-motion clock. Tests cover finite geometry, deterministic seeking and exact cycle reset.

Files: `public/landing/assets/projects/023-pestoverflow-v1.png`, `public/landing/artifacts/pestoverflow.js`, `public/landing/artifacts/early-exhibit-studio.js`.

A physically believable finely crafted cutaway utility room on a rounded pale limestone rectangular plinth: left a terracotta brick rear wall with brass plumbing and a metal mesh ventilation grille; on the floor a small realistically detailed brown mouse approaches a black sensor aperture. Center a large open ivory electronics enclosure with detailed dark green Raspberry Pi circuit board, tiny gold connector pins, silver ports, visible copper and black wires, a white faceted PIR dome and a small black camera lens mounted on a brass bracket. Right a tall upright charcoal phone shows one restrained coral alert symbol and a small map location pin in relief, plus three cream message lines. A thin brass conduit physically connects device to phone with small coral signal markers. Clear left-to-right detection-to-notification story. Real fine grain, beveled edges, tiny screws, layered detail. Warm soft directional studio lighting, convincing soft contact shadows, dark warm brown seamless background, close three-quarter elevated front view, whole base visible with comfortable margins, no outside text, no random floating objects, no cartoon humans, no plastic toy look. A small inset front plaque reads exactly 'PestOverflow'.

## Refinement, 23 October 2026

Rebuilt the solid electronics plate as an open enclosure with a rear pan, thick shell walls, front gasket, hinges, ventilation, mounting feet and PCB standoffs. GPIO pins, IC legs, solder lands, capacitors, USB ports, Fresnel-style faceted sensor dome and a coated camera lens give the device real depth. Recesses and bevels catch the existing warm studio lighting; enamel, mineral stone and reflective lenses remain distinct. Added floor joints and a masonry footing.

Replaced the intersecting ellipsoid mouse with `pestoverflow-mouse.js`: a continuous sculpted coat and tapered muzzle, fine directional fur bump, cupped ears, glassy inset eyes, tapered tail and whiskers. Four articulated paws use stance/swing motion only during traversal and stay planted during the detection hold. SMS text now belongs to the animated notification and appears only after the signal; the notification eases into place and retracts before reset. A lower camera elevation makes the enclosure and electronics easier to read.

Regression coverage verifies a genuine half-unit cavity via raycasts, preserved coat colour attributes, resting foot positions, active gait, deterministic seeking and an exact cycle reset. Fresh browser visual verification is unavailable because local browser control is disabled; the previous artwork is retained as the composition reference.
