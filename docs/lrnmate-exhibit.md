# LrnMate exhibit

Research: https://github.com/arav-behl/LrnMate (README, BasicTable.js, QuizScreen.js), https://www.instagram.com/p/C8MapmzSGnB/ (June 14, 2024).

The README proposes workplace document search, snippets/video, quizzes, knowledge transfer and on-premise privacy. The React prototype contains a question-bank list, quiz flow and results based on bundled questions. The Instagram caption explicitly says the team underestimated complexity and only completed the UI during the hackathon. Do not describe the concept artwork as evidence of a completed AI pipeline or deployed private enterprise product.

Visual direction: warm limestone knowledge studio, layered books and binder, blue lesson ribbon, text/video/quiz relief cards, learner and mentor at a fluted desk, laptop, reference book, brass lamp, and feedback arc. The 3D version uses real geometry, tactile procedural materials, studio lighting, subtle card/head/arm animation, and the gallery's shared pause/reset/reduced-motion clock.

Artwork matching pass: staggered full-height library with printed covers and red ring binder; a continuous woven ribbon under three larger relief cards; landscape video, quiz radio rows and book icon; enlarged fluted study desk, seated learner, mentor with glasses, chair, books and brass lamp; sparse blue beads on a directional brass return arc; canvas-drawn stone/cloth/print surfaces and readable LrnMate plaque. Component bounds improve the initial camera framing. Characters remain stylized procedural sculptures, rather than reproductions of the artwork's realistic human figures.

Sculpture and motion pass: replaced round toy figures with adult cross-section torsos, tapered sleeves/trousers, shaped jaws and noses, narrow eyelids, swept hair, fingers, collars and lapels. The learner sits at a writing height; the mentor gestures above the desk. A dedicated LrnMate studio profile adds warm key/rim lighting and balanced fill. Stone now has porous bump detail; ribbon and binder have distinct surface relief. The shared animation clock drives writing/teaching gestures, head motion, fabric deformation, floating cards and two beads travelling along the return arc. Ribbon geometry is excluded from static batching so its animated vertices remain live. Verified finite geometry, deterministic animation, pause/resume, Astro check and production build.

Surface refinement: sleeves, trousers and torso now use interpolated cross-sections instead of hard cylinder ends. Lapels are thin bevelled fabric panels; facial relief is integrated into the head surface and hair has a continuous cap. LrnMate has a 32-ray contact-occlusion bake (`lrnmate-ao-v1.bin`), validated against all 112 browser mesh records, while retaining live animated lighting/shadows. Rebuild with `node scripts/bake-storybook-ao.mjs --model=lrnmate --distance=0.28 --rays=32 --out=public/landing/assets/lrnmate-ao-v1.bin` after geometry changes. A warm studio backdrop and lower fill light separate surfaces more clearly.
